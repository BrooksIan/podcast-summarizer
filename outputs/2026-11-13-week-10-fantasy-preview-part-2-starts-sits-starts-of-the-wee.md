## Episode Summary
This episode of the podcast dives into Week 10 fantasy football lineup decisions, offering injury updates, player performance breakdowns, and strategic recommendations. It covers key players to start, sit, or monitor, along with matchup-based insights to help fantasy managers make informed decisions.

## Key Points
- Lamar Jackson is expected to miss 6-10 weeks, making Jacoby Brissett a potential fantasy starter if the Ravens stay in playoff contention.
- Jaden Daniels had a strong Week 10 debut with 11 carries and 20.76 fantasy points, while Cam Scadaboo scored 27.2 points with two touchdowns.
- Mike Evans was removed from projections, while Ramondre Stevenson and A.J. Brown are being monitored for potential returns.
- Woody Marks and David Montgomery have uncertain roles in the Texans’ backfield, with opposite practice report trends.
- Jaden Daniels is considered a borderline QB2 due to his rushing ability, while Rasheed Rice remains a viable WR2 despite recent production dips.
- The Chiefs have allowed tight ends to score 14.5+ fantasy points in the first four weeks but none have topped nine points in the last four weeks.
- Jacoby Myers is highlighted as a low-end WR2/flex due to a favorable matchup against the Titans.
- Cam Ward remains unreliable despite a career-high game, with only two 20+ point performances in eight starts.
- Ashton Gentry is the primary pass-catching back for the Raiders, while Zach Charbonnet is a strong start against them.
- The Rams’ WR room is shallow, with Kanata Mumfield leading in catches (23) among the options.
- Matthew Stafford is not recommended due to poor recent performances, lack of rushing ability, and injuries to key receivers.
- A.J. Brown is expected to play against the Lions and is recommended as a start if active.
- Javante Williams is a strong play against the 49ers due to their struggles against pass-catching RBs.
- Dak Prescott has three straight games with 19+ fantasy points and is a viable start this week.
- DK Metcalf is recommended to be benched due to CB DJ Turner’s shadow coverage, while the Bengals’ core players are recommended starts.
- Zay Flowers has a ~30% target share, making him a must-play fantasy asset with Jacoby Brissett at QB.
- Jacoby Brissett is described as a solid NFL starter but not a high-ceiling QB, though his ability to target Flowers is key.
- Mark Andrews has been underperforming, and the Ravens’ decision to pay him over Isaiah Likely is criticized.
- Recommended "Starts of the Week" include Jared Goff, Chuba Hubbard, DJ Moore, Hunter Henry, Trevor Lawrence, and Parker Washington.
