## Episode Summary
This episode of *Fantasy Focus* covers Week 11 waiver wire priorities, key fantasy football adds, and lighthearted banter among the hosts. The hosts discuss limited waiver options, breakout candidates, and personal updates while offering strategic advice for fantasy managers.

## Key Points
- The Chargers broke an 0-8 streak, but concerns linger about their offensive consistency and Derrick Henry’s underutilization (10 carries for 10 yards).
- Waiver wire options are slim this week, with most players projected around 91 fantasy points, making it hard to significantly improve rosters.
- **Key waiver adds** include:
  - **Cam Ward (QB)**: Strong streaming option with recent solid performances (19+ fantasy points in two straight games) and a favorable matchup against the Cowboys.
  - **Jordan Tyson (WR)**: Top waiver add with increased targets in the Saints' offense, making him a high-upside flex option.
  - **Amari DiMarcato (RB)**: Speculative add if David Montgomery (Texans) is out, given Houston’s poor RB production.
  - **Xavier Hutchinson (WR)**: Short-term flex option with increased targets and TD upside in the Jets' offense.
  - **Jawan Jennings (WR)**: Potential flex play in a revenge game against the 49ers, with increased volume expected with Justin Jefferson out.
- Other notable mentions include **Michael Mayer (TE)** (desperation add if Brock Bowers is out) and **DeAndre Swift (RB)**, who remains a strong play despite Kyle Manungai’s return.
- Six teams are on bye, creating urgency to fill roster spots with viable options.
- The hosts discuss additional waiver wire options like **Luther Burden III (WR, Bears)**, **Parker Washington (WR, Jaguars)**, and **DJ Moore (WR, Commanders)**, analyzing their rest-of-season schedules.
- The episode includes lighthearted segments like a "Deal or No Deal"-style game, stadium food eliminations, and sitcom discussions.
